As a child I used to watch fairy tales about a little bee or an ant. The little bee flew out every morning and gathered honey. The ant could fix anything. They were such typical Czech fairy tales. Back then it was perfectly normal for butterflies to flutter in the garden and for trees to be full of bees. The air was full of flying insects.

Now, as an old man, I must tell you, child, that a powerful sorcerer has destroyed the insects. You see, I lived before the internet. When I was growing up, mobile phones were not common. All that remains are old memories of fairy tales, of a world where insects flew. The world is poisoned by a powerful sorcerer who sells people poison and they spray it on their fields. Poison that kills insects. The water is poisoned with chemicals and microplastics. Insects have lost the places where they live. And we did not even notice, because we had no time to go out into nature.
